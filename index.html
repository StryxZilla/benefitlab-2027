METHODOLOGY & SOURCES

Designed for decisions, not surprises.

What is modeled: 2027 employee premiums, Dell wellness discounts and working-spouse surcharge, Cedar medical opt-out credit, employer HSA deposits, individual/family deductibles, copays, coinsurance and in-network out-of-pocket caps.

What is illustrative: Visit counts and negotiated service charges. Preventive well-child visits and recommended vaccines are modeled as $0 member cost. Services are processed in a consistent order; actual claim dates and service classifications can change results.

HSA accounting: "Net economic" subtracts employer HSA deposits as retained savings value; "Cash paid" does not. Your own HSA contributions are not a medical cost or a rebate and are not modeled here.

Important limitations: Only covered, in-network care. Excludes out-of-network and noncovered charges, separate facility fees beyond the specified assumptions, employer payroll taxes, personal HSA tax deductions, coordination-of-benefits rules and plan-specific special programs.

Sources: Cedar Cares 2027 Benefits Guide pp. 14–15 and Cigna HDHP/PPO summaries; Dell 2027 Benefits Summary pp. 2–5, 11 and 2027 Enrollment Guide pp. 9–12; the uploaded family comparison workbook. Planning model only—verify exact carrier claim rules before electing.
